Health Care Solutions

General Surgery

our General Surgery department provides comprehensive surgical care for a wide range of conditions using modern techniques and a patient-centred approach. Our experienced surgical team offers treatment for appendix surgery, hernia repair, hydrocele, fissure, fistula, varicose veins, breast lumps and breast cancer, parotid gland surgery, gall bladder surgery, and many other surgical conditions. From accurate diagnosis and treatment planning to surgery and post-operative care, we are committed to providing safe, compassionate, and personalized care focused on your comfort and recovery.

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

our Obstetrics & Gynaecology department provides comprehensive and compassionate healthcare for women at every stage of life. Our experienced team offers expert care for pregnancy and childbirth, antenatal and postnatal care, high-risk pregnancies, infertility, PCOS, menstrual disorders, menopause, uterine and ovarian conditions, fibroids, endometriosis, ovarian cysts, and other gynaecological concerns. With personalized treatment plans, modern medical facilities, and a strong focus on maternal and reproductive health, we are committed to supporting every woman with safe, trusted, and quality care throughout her healthcare journey.

Read More

Orthopedics

Our Orthopaedics department provides comprehensive care for conditions affecting the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and spine. Our experienced orthopaedic specialists offer personalized diagnosis and treatment for fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, joint pain, ligament and tendon problems, and other musculoskeletal conditions. With advanced diagnostic facilities, modern treatment options, and dedicated rehabilitation support, we focus on relieving pain, restoring mobility, and helping patients return to an active and healthy life.
